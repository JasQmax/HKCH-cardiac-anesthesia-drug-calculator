FLACC Behavioural Pain Scale

For infants/children 0–7 years or non-verbal/cognitively impaired patients. Observe for 1–5 minutes. Score each category 0–2.

FLACC Score
Interpretation:
  • 0: Relaxed & comfortable — no pain intervention needed
  • 1–3: Mild discomfort — consider non-pharmacological interventions, reassess
  • 4–6: Moderate pain — consider analgesia, reassess after intervention
  • 7–10: Severe pain/discomfort — analgesia indicated, escalate if needed
Reference: Merkel SI, et al. The FLACC: a behavioral scale for scoring postoperative pain in young children. Pediatr Nurs 1997;23(3):293-7.

Instructions for use:
  1. Explain to the child that each face shows a person who has no pain (hurt), some, or a lot of pain.
  2. Point to each face and describe: "This face has no hurt... this face has a little bit of hurt... this face has the worst hurt."
  3. Ask the child to choose the face that best describes how much hurt they have right now.
Interpretation & Action:
  • 0: No pain — continue to monitor
  • 2: Mild pain — non-pharmacological measures, consider simple analgesia
  • 4: Mild–moderate pain — simple analgesia (paracetamol, NSAIDs)
  • 6: Moderate pain — combination analgesia, consider weak opioid
  • 8–10: Severe pain — strong analgesia indicated, escalate care
Reference: Wong-Baker FACES Foundation. wongbakerfaces.org. Used with permission.

HK2020 P50 weight-for-age (for < 50 kg, obese/overweight branch):
Ideal body weight is interpolated from the Hong Kong Department of Health 2020 Growth Reference 50th-percentile weight-for-age charts, using the patient's exact age (years + months) and sex.

Devine formula (for ≥ 50 kg branch, Fentanyl only):
Male IBW (kg) = 50 + 2.3 × (height in inches − 60)
Female IBW (kg) = 45.5 + 2.3 × (height in inches − 60)
Height entered in cm is auto-converted to inches for this calculation. Devine is classically an adult formula — used here only for patients ≥ 50 kg.

Final Dosing Weight decision rule (applied after IBW calculation):
  • If IBW > BW: use BW
  • If IBW < BW and BW < 140 kg: use Adjusted Body Weight (ABW) = IBW + 0.4 × (BW − IBW)
  • If ABW > 100 kg, or BW ≥ 140 kg: cap dosing weight at 100 kg
